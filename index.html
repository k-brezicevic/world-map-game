About

A geography game where the goal is to find all 202 countries on the world map, one at a time.

See the Countries and Territories tab in the info panel for what counts as a country in this game.

  • A country name is displayed at the top.
  • Find the country on the map and double-click it.
  • Alternatively, single-click the country and press Enter on the keyboard.
  • If the answer is correct, the country turns green.
  • If the answer is wrong, then the correct country briefly flashes green, and then stays red. The country that was clicked turns back from orange to normal color.
  • Skip button – shows the next country while the skipped one moves to the end of the list and comes up later.
  • Don't know button – shows where the country is and counts it as a miss.
  • Zoom with the scroll wheel or the +/− keys, and drag to move the map.
  • The scoreboard in the top-left corner shows correct answers, misses and countries remaining.

This map treats 202 areas as countries:

  • All 193 member states of the United Nations,
  • Palestine and Vatican City – the two UN observer states,
  • Taiwan – a de facto sovereign state,
  • Kosovo – a partially recognized sovereign state,
  • Greenland, Puerto Rico, the Falkland Islands, French Guiana and New Caledonia – larger territories with a distinct geographic identity.
